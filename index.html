Climate shocks and sovereign debt risk

Quantitative research project mentored by a J.P. Morgan risk quant, March to July 2026

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • pandas
  • CNN, LSTM, attention
  • Vector autoregression
The question
El Niño and La Niña bring droughts and floods to both sides of the Pacific. Do those shocks show up in a country's ability to service its debt?
What I built
A statistical model of how the cycle affects local climate, using ERA5-Land data and the Oceanic Niño Index. A multi-branch CNN, LSTM and attention network that reads spatial climate grids plus 24 months of history to estimate the chance of crop-yield decline. Vector autoregression models that trace climate shocks through to inflation and GDP across 12 emerging markets.
Key lesson
Strong in-sample results do not guarantee reliable forecasts. The project sharpened my approach to overfitting, data preparation and model validation.
